Motivation

Good software removes work nobody should have to do by hand.

What draws me to software engineering is the moment a process that took weeks runs in hours. AIchitect started from that idea. Developers pay agencies tens of thousands of euros for a sales brochure that is largely built from data already contained in the architect’s plans. My architecture internship showed me how much precision those plans carry, and my year in San Francisco showed me how quickly a small team can build something useful with AI. At CODE I am learning to do this properly, with clean architecture, reliable testing, and products that hold up in everyday use.

My interest in computer science started at fifteen, when I began reading books about it on my own, and it grew in my advanced computer science course at school. It is also the reason I started learning Chinese, because computer science carries enormous weight in the Chinese market and I wanted a way to stand out.

Before Berlin I spent a year in San Francisco, where I picked up a very specific obsession with building things that do not exist yet. I came home and started making them.

I build in public and write the honest version of it. The wins, the mess, and everything I learn about AI, ambition, and figuring it out without a blueprint.
